HOW WE RANK

Ranked on what you would actually earn

We never rank on headline marketing. Each card is scored on the rewards it earns on your spend, how well it fits your transfer partners and fee limit, and how closely it matches your goals.

  1. Estimate. Your monthly spend is split across your categories and priced at each card's standard rates.
  2. Compare. Cards are compared by rupee value where we have one, because a point from one bank is not worth the same as another's.
  3. Fit. Partners, annual fee and goals adjust the order. Cards with missing data are still shown, marked as partial.

Frequently asked questions

How does the tool rank cards?

It estimates the rewards each card earns on your monthly spend across the categories you choose, then weighs that with how well the card fits your preferred transfer partners, annual fee limit and goals. Cards with more complete data rank slightly higher.

Are points from different banks comparable?

Not directly. A point from one bank is worth a different amount than a point from another, so we compare cards by the rupee value of what they earn wherever we have a published value, and show estimated points in each card's own currency.

Why do some cards say partial data?

We only show what we have verified from issuer documents. If a card's earn rate, annual fee or transfer partners are not yet recorded, the card is still listed but marked as partial so you know what is missing.

Do the estimates include caps and exclusions?

No. Estimates use each card's standard earn rates and split your spend evenly across the categories you pick. Monthly caps, excluded merchants and time-limited offers are not applied, so treat results as a guide and check the issuer's terms.
