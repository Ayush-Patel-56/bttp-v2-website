    HOW IT WORKS

    From award chart to card points

    An airline's award chart tells you the miles a flight costs. Your bank's transfer ratio tells you how many card points it takes to buy those miles. We join the two, then check them against your balance.

    1. Price. We look up the partner's miles for your route, cabin and award type.
    2. Convert. Miles are multiplied by your card's transfer ratio and rounded to the transfer rules.
    3. Check. We compare the result with your balance and show transfer minimums, limits and timing.

    card points = partner miles × (card points ÷ partner points)

    Award charts change without notice and availability is never guaranteed. Always confirm the price and seat on the airline's own site before transferring points, because transfers cannot be reversed.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many credit card points do I need for a flight?

    Find the partner miles the flight costs on the airline's award chart, then convert them to card points using your bank's transfer ratio. For example, a 5:2 ratio means 5 card points become 2 partner miles, so 20,000 miles needs 50,000 card points. This planner does both steps and checks your balance.

    What are Saver and Advantage awards?

    KrisFlyer prices some Singapore Airlines award seats at a lower Saver rate and others at a higher Advantage rate. Saver seats are limited, so the planner shows both prices.

    Do the point costs include taxes and fees?

    No. Award charts show the points or miles only. Airlines and hotels usually charge taxes, fees and sometimes fuel surcharges on top, paid in cash.

    Why is my card or airline not listed?

    The planner covers transfer partners whose award charts we have verified and that can be priced by region: KrisFlyer, Maharaja Club, Finnair Plus and Wyndham Rewards. Distance-based charts such as Aeroplan and Qantas, and others, are being added.
